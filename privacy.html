What we collect

When you sign up: your phone number, your email, which option you picked (the free waitlist, the Crawler Kit or the Investor Edition), the date and time, and the page that sent you (for example, TikTok). If you buy, Stripe processes the payment; we receive your email and the payment status, never your card number.

When you visit: anonymous counts only. The page adds one to a daily tally for the source tag in the link you came from (for example, tiktok) when it loads, when each video has been on screen for a few seconds, when you reach the sign-up form, and when you sign up. Nothing that identifies you is sent with these counts: no phone, email, cookie, IP address or device ID.

What we use it for

  • Texts and emails about the web crawler: launch news, access, and new videos. Up to 4 messages a month.
  • Delivering what people paid for.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your phone number or email. Mobile numbers and text-message consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing. We only share data with the services that run this list (Google, which stores the sign-up sheet; Stripe, which takes payments; and the text or email service used to send messages).

Opting out
